My perspective

The opportunity for Voyage AI is not simply to increase awareness of its embedding and reranking models. It is to help developers discover the technology, experience its value, integrate it into applications, and expand usage as workloads mature.

A signup is not necessarily activation; activation is not necessarily adoption.

My growth thesis

Connect lifecycle marketing to product behavior through three priorities:

  1. 01Accelerate first value
  2. 02Convert experimentation into sustained adoption
  3. 03Identify expansion opportunities from usage signals
01

Accelerate developer activation

ObjectiveReduce friction between discovery, signup, and the first meaningful product outcome.

  • Partner with Product, Product Marketing, and Builder Relations to map developer journeys and identify drop-off points.
  • Segment by acquisition source, use case, and available onboarding behavior; test relevant documentation, tutorials, and examples.
  • Trigger lifecycle journeys when developers register but do not complete meaningful activation steps.
MeasureSignup-to-activation rate, time to first successful API request, onboarding completion, and activation by segment.

02

Convert experimentation into sustained adoption

ObjectiveHelp developers move from evaluation to consistent use in real applications.

  • Build use-case-specific lifecycle journeys for embeddings, reranking, and retrieval workflows.
  • Test product education, launch communications, and re-engagement programs informed by observed usage and friction.
  • Prioritize experiments by expected impact, measurable outcomes, and implementation effort.
MeasureActivated-user retention, repeat API usage, feature adoption, cohort engagement, and progression toward production workloads.
03

Build a behavior-driven expansion engine

ObjectiveIdentify relevant opportunities to expand adoption and workloads among existing users and accounts.

  • Segment accounts by product usage, engagement, and adoption maturity.
  • Introduce complementary capabilities, such as reranking, where relevant to existing workflows.
  • Coordinate personalized expansion programs with Product Marketing and customer-facing teams.
MeasureIncremental API usage, expansion adoption, feature penetration, retained usage, and expansion revenue.
