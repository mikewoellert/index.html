First 90 Days

Diagnose, align, build, then scale

Transparent progress and a repeatable operating rhythm — not an unrealistic promise to transform the funnel in one quarter.

Days 1–30 · Diagnose & Align

  • Review ICP, segmentation, campaigns, website conversion paths, HubSpot lifecycle, scoring, nurture, attribution, and paid media
  • Meet with marketing, sales, product, SMEs, and customer-facing stakeholders
  • Identify one or two quick wins — a landing page, a nurture sequence, or conversion tracking
  • Ship and measure at least one improvement; document the funnel baseline

Days 31–60 · Build Repeatable Programs

  • Develop a prioritized campaign roadmap tied to audience segments and business goals
  • Build or improve one integrated campaign — content, landing page, distribution, nurture, tracking, and sales follow-up
  • Create reusable templates for briefs, landing pages, nurture, ad variants, and reporting

Days 61–90 · Optimize & Scale

  • Evaluate conversion by audience, source, campaign, landing page, and lifecycle stage
  • Identify where content, qualification, nurture, or sales alignment most improves results
  • Present a 90-day readout: what shipped, what changed, and recommended priorities

Supporting the VP of Marketing

Reducing coordination, not adding to it

My goal would be to reduce the executional coordination and follow-up required from the VP of Marketing — bringing recommendations with context, maintaining a visible campaign and testing backlog, communicating risks early, and owning projects through launch and analysis.

I'd make it easy to answer five questions: What are we trying to accomplish? Who are we targeting? What is being executed? What is working or not working? What decision or support is needed?
